# Smart Museum Artifact Conservation System

## Task 2: Complete Operation Schemas

| #  | Operation                                    | Purpose                                                         | Preconditions                                                                   | Inputs                                                                                            | Processing                                                                                                | Outputs                                        | Postconditions                                                                                     | Exceptions                                          |
| -- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | **Perform Sensor Self-Check**                | Verify that all essential sensors are working correctly.        | Chamber is powered on; essential sensors are connected.                         | Temperature, humidity, light, vibration and door sensors                                          | Test each sensor, check responses, identify faulty sensors, generate result.                              | Sensor self-check result                       | All essential sensors are confirmed working; system can proceed to monitoring.                     | One or more essential sensors fail.                 |
| 2  | **Register Artifact**                        | Record the identification information of the artifact.          | Artifact is placed inside the chamber.                                          | Artifact ID, artifact identification information                                                  | Receive information, validate it, store artifact record.                                                  | Artifact record                                | Artifact is successfully registered.                                                               | Invalid or missing artifact information.            |
| 3  | **Load Environmental Profile**               | Load the environmental requirements specified for the artifact. | Artifact is registered.                                                         | Required temperature range, humidity range, permitted vibration level, other environmental limits | Retrieve requirements, validate limits, store environmental profile.                                      | Loaded environmental profile                   | Required environmental limits are available for monitoring.                                        | Environmental profile is missing or invalid.        |
| 4  | **Monitor Environmental Conditions**         | Continuously monitor the chamber environment.                   | Sensors are working; artifact is inside the chamber.                            | Temperature, humidity, light exposure, vibration, door status                                     | Read sensor values, record conditions, compare readings with limits, detect abnormal conditions.          | Current environmental status                   | Environmental conditions are continuously monitored.                                               | Sensor reading is unavailable or invalid.           |
| 5  | **Check Chamber Door Status**                | Determine whether the chamber door is open or closed.           | Door sensor is working.                                                         | Door sensor reading                                                                               | Read sensor, determine current status, report open/closed condition.                                      | Door status                                    | System knows the current chamber door condition.                                                   | Door sensor failure.                                |
| 6  | **Compare Temperature with Required Limits** | Determine whether temperature is within the permitted range.    | Environmental profile is loaded; temperature sensor is working.                 | Current temperature, minimum temperature, maximum temperature                                     | Read temperature, compare with minimum and maximum limits, determine normal/abnormal status.              | Temperature status                             | System determines whether temperature correction is required.                                      | Temperature sensor failure.                         |
| 7  | **Compare Humidity with Required Limits**    | Determine whether humidity is within the permitted range.       | Environmental profile is loaded; humidity sensor is working.                    | Current humidity, minimum humidity, maximum humidity                                              | Read humidity, compare with minimum and maximum limits, determine normal/abnormal status.                 | Humidity status                                | System determines whether humidity correction is required.                                         | Humidity sensor failure.                            |
| 8  | **Issue Temperature Correction Command**     | Correct temperature when it moves outside the permitted range.  | Temperature is outside required range; temperature-control device is available. | Current temperature, required temperature range                                                   | Determine required correction, activate control mechanism, apply correction command.                      | Temperature correction command                 | Temperature correction has been attempted; verification starts.                                    | Temperature-control device is unavailable.          |
| 9  | **Issue Humidity Correction Command**        | Correct humidity when it moves outside the permitted range.     | Humidity is outside required range; humidity-control device is available.       | Current humidity, required humidity range                                                         | Determine required correction, activate control mechanism, apply correction command.                      | Humidity correction command                    | Humidity correction has been attempted; verification starts.                                       | Humidity-control device is unavailable.             |
| 10 | **Verify Temperature Recovery**              | Verify that temperature has returned to the permitted range.    | Temperature correction command has been issued.                                 | Current temperature, required temperature range, allowed recovery period                          | Read temperature, compare with required range, monitor during recovery period, determine recovery status. | Recovery successful or failed                  | Successful recovery allows normal conservation; failed recovery may require protection response.   | Temperature does not recover within allowed period. |
| 11 | **Verify Humidity Recovery**                 | Verify that humidity has returned to the permitted range.       | Humidity correction command has been issued.                                    | Current humidity, required humidity range, allowed recovery period                                | Read humidity, compare with required range, monitor during recovery period, determine recovery status.    | Recovery successful or failed                  | Successful recovery allows normal conservation; failed recovery may require protection response.   | Humidity does not recover within allowed period.    |
| 12 | **Detect Significant Vibration**             | Detect vibration that may put the artifact at risk.             | Artifact is inside chamber; vibration sensor is working.                        | Current vibration level, permitted vibration threshold                                            | Read vibration level, compare with threshold, determine whether significant vibration is present.         | Vibration detected or no significant vibration | If significant vibration is detected, activities that may increase artifact risk can be suspended. | Vibration sensor failure.                           |
